SELLER BUSINESS MODELS

Marketplace vs. Vendor

Commercial terms vary by retailer and contract. This comparison is a general model overview, not a live vendor program.

Third-party marketplace (3P)

You sell directly to buyers, act as merchant of record, set your offer price, and manage your product listing and available stock. Choose FBA or FBM per listing.

Interactive demo available

Direct vendor / wholesale (1P)

You supply products wholesale to the retailer under agreed terms. The retailer typically owns retail inventory and controls the consumer-facing offer; purchasing and fulfillment follow the vendor agreement.
